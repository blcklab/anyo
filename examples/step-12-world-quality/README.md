# Step 12 — Integrated World Quality

Asterfall Research Garden is the integrated S25 visual-foundation showcase. It is intentionally one coherent twilight world rather than a row of feature-test objects.

It combines only accepted generic systems: procedural environment sky/stars/clouds, authored reusable mesh clouds, atmosphere, generic water materials, deterministic particles, alpha-masked thin vegetation, deterministic repeat variation, procedural rock geometry, PBR architecture, glass/metal/wood/concrete, material micro-detail and height detail, wet/clearcoated surfaces, shadows, SSAO and bloom.

Decals and generic volumes remain deliberately deferred per Steps 7 and 11. No Step 12 engine subsystem is introduced.
